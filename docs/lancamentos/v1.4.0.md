Retro Computer 1.4.0 adds the Classic Mac theme, inspired by Mac OS 9, and the Mac Plus, an emulator for the classic Macintosh games.

## New

- **The Classic Mac theme**: Control Panel › Appearance › **Theme** now offers the Classic PC (the usual one) and the Classic Mac, each with a small preview. Each user has their own theme, and switching back is one click.
- **The menu bar at the top of the screen**: the logo on the left opens the Start menu (the logo key too, and About Retro Computer comes first, as in the Mac of the time); the menus of the front window move up to it; with the desktop in front (a click on it), it shows the desktop's own menus: File, Edit, View, Special (with Empty Trash) and Help. On the right, as on the Mac of the time, there are only the clock and the menu of the open programs, which shows the front one, switches between them, hides (minimizes) and shows them again.
- **Platinum windows**: the light gray frame, the striped title with the close box on the left and the zoom and collapse boxes on the right. A double-click on the title collapses the window to its title bar, and another opens it again. The buttons have rounded corners and the thick ring on the default one; the check boxes, radio buttons, pop-up menus, tabs, scroll bars and progress bars follow the same style, with the lavender highlight.
- **The Control Strip**: in the bottom left corner, the programs pinned to the taskbar, the search magnifier (a click opens the box) and the tray icons (network, volume, battery, notifications). As on the Mac of the time, it starts collapsed, with only its end in view: a click on the end opens it (Control Panel › Taskbar and Start Menu keeps it open).
- **The desktop**: the icons start from the top right corner and the Trash sits in the bottom corner (each theme keeps its own positions). The usual background of one theme becomes the other's; a background you chose stays as it is.
- **Its own icons**: folders, documents, the disk, the Trash, the programs and the alerts, drawn pixel by pixel by the project in the style of the time. As always, nothing comes from Apple: the Retro Computer logo takes the place of the apple.
- **Its own startup**: a box with the logo and the progress bar, and the icons appearing one by one along the bottom.
- **The Mac Plus** (Start › Programs › Accessories), for the classic Macintosh games such as SimAnt: the PCE.js emulator in a window, in black and white and without the internet, built like the DOS one (an isolated frame, unmodified, GPL-2.0). Nothing from Apple comes with Retro Computer: put your Mac's ROM image (the 128 KB `.rom` file of a Mac Plus; the 256 KB one of a Mac SE and the 512 KB one of a Mac Classic also work), the System floppy (System 6 or 7) and your game floppies (`.dsk`, `.img` or `.image`, from Disk Copy 4.2) in the C:\Mac folder. The disk with System or Boot in its name goes into the first drive; double-clicking a `.dsk` or `.image` file starts the Mac with it. What the Mac writes goes back to the disk when the window closes (and every two minutes), and the version from before the Mac was started is kept. Until the ROM and a disk are there, the window says what is missing, with a button that opens the folder.

## Improved

- **The Shut Down icon** of the Classic PC theme (Start menu and the Shut Down box) is now the project's own desktop computer, with the screen off, the power symbol and the red lights; the old one looked like a laptop. The Program Store box is redrawn in three-quarter view: the open cardboard box with its flaps and the floppy disk coming out of it.
- Changing the theme through Claude (the MCP server) also switches the usual background, as the Control Panel does.
- The Trash (32 pixels) and Documents (16 pixels) icons of the Classic PC theme have their whole outline again.
- **The tray icons, one by one**: Control Panel › Taskbar and Start Menu › Tray icons shows or hides the network, the volume, the battery, Bluetooth and the notifications, in both themes (all of them stay in view by default).

## Fixed

- The Help showed the C:\DOS folder as "C:DOS".
