Retro Computer 1.0.1 fixes what came up in the first days of use of 1.0.

## Fixes

- **Drag and drop onto folders and the Trash**: dropping files on a folder icon, on the Trash or inside an open folder could be ignored with a real mouse (on Windows and on Linux). Hovering a folder changed the size of its name, the pointer ended up over another part of the icon, and that last notice before the drop refused it. Now both the entering and the hovering accept the drop, and the highlight only changes the colors.
- **Renaming on the desktop**: the name being typed was white on a white box. It is black now, as in the folder windows.
- **Explorer and Browser toolbars**: in a narrow window, the buttons no longer get cut ("Volt", "Acim"); when they do not fit, the bar shows only the icons (the name stays in the tooltip), and the names come back when the window is wider.
- **Signing in to Google** in the Retro Computer Browser and in E-mail: Google refused the login ("This browser or app may not be secure"). On the Google sign-in pages, the Browser now presents itself as Firefox, as other apps that gather websites do; everywhere else it stays as Chrome.
