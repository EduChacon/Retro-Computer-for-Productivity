# Retro Computer 2.6.0

- Breakout now has a campaign of 24 original levels with four color themes, reinforced bricks, indestructible steel obstacles and explosive chain reactions. Clearing a level unlocks the next, with a campaign victory after level 24.
- Eight falling power-ups add a wide paddle, multiball, laser, slow ball, magnetic paddle, bottom shield, fireball and extra lives. Consecutive brick breaks build a score multiplier. Power-up timers freeze while paused; multiball costs a life only when the last ball is lost.
- A level picker shows previews of unlocked stages. Best scores from older versions are preserved, and level unlocks and sound preferences survive closing the app. Ongoing games restart with three lives. Mouse, touch, arrows and A/D move the paddle; Space launches or fires, P/Escape pauses and F2 starts again.
- The resizable game window keeps the original pixel-style artwork and synthesized sound on Windows, macOS and Linux. Switching windows or minimizing pauses play and releases held controls. Reduced-motion preferences disable particles and ball trails. Help and game controls are available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
