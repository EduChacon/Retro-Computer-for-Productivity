Retro Computer 2.0.8 blocks ads and trackers in the Browser.

## New

- **Ad and tracker blocking in the Browser.** Requests to the known ad and tracking networks (Google Ads, Taboola, Outbrain, Criteo, Amazon and others) are cancelled, and the empty spaces the ads would leave disappear from the page. The list is Retro Computer's own and nothing is downloaded. The page you open is never blocked, only what it loads. It is on by default: **View › Block ads** in the Browser, or Control Panel › Security Center › Privacy, turns it on and off. If a site does not work well, **View › Allow ads on this site** leaves just that site out and reloads the page. Ads inside YouTube videos, which come from YouTube itself, may still play. With Firefox as the browser, Firefox's own tracking protection is turned on instead.
