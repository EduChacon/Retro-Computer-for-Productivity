Retro Computer 1.9.0 brings a dark mode that turns on by itself at sunset, color emoji drawn the same way everywhere and a new phone icon.

## New

- **Dark mode at night** (Control Panel › Appearance › Theme › Color scheme › Switch at night to): during the day the chosen scheme stays, and at night another one (one of the dark ones, such as Night), each with its usual background. The night goes from sunset to sunrise, worked out for the reference city of the computer's time zone (São Paulo, for America/Sao_Paulo), without asking for your location; today's times appear beside the option, and fixed hours can be chosen instead. When the computer was off at sunset or sunrise, the right scheme comes back when it starts. Claude can turn it on too.
- **Night light from sunset to sunrise** (Control Panel › Appearance › Effects), with the same sunset and sunrise, besides fixed hours and all day.

## Changed

- **Emoji** now use the Noto Color Emoji font (Google, SIL Open Font License 1.1), which comes with Retro Computer: the emoji panel shows exactly what appears in the text, and the emoji in Notepad, in the fields and in the sticky notes look the same on Windows, macOS and Linux. Ordinary text stays in the period font; only what it does not have comes out in color. The panel now has 1,905 emoji (it had 1,752), including the newest ones. The font is split into pieces that load only when one of their emoji appears (about 2 MB in all).
- The **Receive from Phone** icon was redrawn: a dark phone with rounded corners, light and shadow, the side buttons, the speaker and the camera, and a screen with apps.
