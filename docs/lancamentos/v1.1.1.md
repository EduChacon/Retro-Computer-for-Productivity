Retro Computer 1.1.1 fixes what came up in the first hours of 1.1 and adds a few small things.

## Fixes

- **New tabs in the Browser**: while a page was loading, its tab left its place on the tab strip, with the icon above and the close button below. The tab now stays in place, with the loading icon.
- **Full screen of videos inside other sites**: a video from another site embedded in a page (a Vimeo video on a course page, for example) only filled the page area. It now covers the whole screen, and Esc leaves it. Full screen of whole pages, as on YouTube, works as before.

## New

- **Rearrange the favorites bar** by dragging the favorites, as on the taskbar. The order is saved.
- **More pixel sizes**: besides 1, 2 and 3, there are now 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2.5 and 4 (Control Panel › Appearance › Settings). Each option shows the screen area it gives; the whole sizes keep the letters sharper.
