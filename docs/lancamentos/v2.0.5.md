Retro Computer 2.0.5 keeps the picture-in-picture video on top of the desktop, brings window snapping to the Linux programs, and puts the emoji panel on the right-click menu of text boxes.

## Fixed

- **The picture-in-picture video disappeared** (Retro Computer as the system). A click on the desktop, or anywhere in Retro Computer, hid the floating video of Firefox or the Browser behind it. Retro Computer now covers the screen without the full-screen mode of the window manager, so the windows that stay on top (the floating video) really stay on top. The lock screen still covers everything, the floating video included.
- **Window snapping did not work on the Linux programs** (Retro Computer as the system). Dragged by the title to the edge of the screen, the window of Firefox, LibreOffice and the other Linux programs now shows the outline and, when you let go, takes half of the screen; to the top, it is maximized; to a corner, it takes a quarter. Dragged again, it goes back to its previous size. The option is the same as for the windows of Retro Computer (Control Panel › Appearance).

## New

- **Emoji on the right-click menu.** Right-click a text box (in Notepad, in a program, in the Command Prompt or on a page of the Browser) › **Emoji…** opens the emoji panel for it, the same panel as Logo+period.
